About Fathom

Radical humanity.
Radical authenticity.
Radical honesty.

This is how we work: with our clients, with each other, and with the data in front of us.

Why we exist

Thorough. Comprehensive. Decisive.

We built Fathom because research was broken.
And everyone knew it.

We saw teams making decisions without confidence. The data existed. Nobody had turned it into something useful while it still mattered.

Timing.

Reports that take months to land arrive after the call’s already made, and get filed away more often than opened. Most of us have sat in that meeting.

Direction.

Not knowing which customers to prioritize, or where the real opportunity is hiding. That doubt rarely raises a red flag. It stalls the room, or lets whoever’s loudest decide.

Closing that gap, timing or direction, is the job.

We hand back more than findings: a recommendation that lands while it can still change the decision, and a clear read on where to look.

Sometimes, the angle nobody in the room had considered.

What we believe

3 things we don’t compromise on.

Judgment.

Curiosity.

Honesty.

Human judgment is still the job.

We answer the question underneath the question.

We say what we see.

Tools can move fast through information. They can’t recognize what matters, know your company’s politics, or make the call you’d stand behind in a room. That’s still a human job, and we take it seriously.

Clients rarely hand us the real question first. They hand us the version they know how to phrase. Part of the job is finding the one underneath it, and staying curious past the first answer that technically fits.

Even when it challenges the brief, the hypothesis, or the answer everyone in the room was hoping for. A finding you can’t trust is decoration.
