Case 04 Automotive Brand Strategy

The brand hadn’t aged. The words had.

A legacy automotive brand thought it had a relevance problem. What it actually had was two words, freedom and adventure, that had quietly changed meaning underneath it.

Client
Legacy automotive brand
Industry
Automotive
Engagement
Brand relevance study
Method
Qualitative depth interviews
The brief

The question every iconic company eventually runs into: how do you stay relevant without becoming generic?

Newer players were crowding the space. Evolve too much and you alienate the people who made you. Evolve too little and you fade. Every setting on that dial costs something, which is why the room kept circling it.

The path most would have taken

Ask loyalists what they love, ask fence-sitters what is missing, split the difference. That produces a checklist, and a checklist misses the tension.

How we built it

Ask people to rank features and you learn about features. We needed the words underneath them.

01 · Definition

“What does freedom mean now?”

Not what the brand means. What the word means, in the life they are living now.

02 · Lived example

“What did adventure look like in your real life?”

A concrete instance, not an aspiration. That gap is where the real meaning shows.

03 · Projection

“Who would this brand be if it walked into a party?”

People describe a brand’s character far more honestly through a person than a scale.

Hundreds of interviews 5 major U.S. markets Brand lovers and skeptics No feature ranking
What surfaced

Two words moved. They moved the same way.

Freedom stopped meaning the open road and started meaning control over your own time, which most people said they lost around 2020. Adventure stopped being the Instagram version and became a small break from routine.

The reframe

The question in the room: what should we change?

So we changed the question

What is this brand already giving people that they can’t name themselves?

The outcome

Not a reinvention. A remix: heritage held, contemporary cues added in steps small enough not to spook the base, and a doubling down on control, customization and everyday adventure. Other brands carry you to the adventure. This one is the adventure.
