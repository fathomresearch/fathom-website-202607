The setup

They wanted a health check. They expected to pass.

{{SETUP_LEAD}} — the brand was doing well, and the brief for a diagnostic in that situation is really a request for reassurance with charts. Score generously, flag a few tidy improvements, confirm that the plan is working.

An honest grade is the entire value of an audit, and it is the one thing a friendly one won’t give you. The moment the people being graded are in the room, scores drift upward, weaknesses become “opportunities,” and the report says what everyone already believed. A diagnostic that tells you what you hoped to hear has cost you money to learn nothing.

So we graded it the way an outsider would — against the category and the best in it, not against last year’s version of itself — and the clean bill of health did not survive contact with the benchmark.

How a grade is defined

A grade is only honest if it means the same thing every time.

Sample rubric · Ease of use
A
A first-time user finishes the core task on the first try, with no help and no dead ends.
B
Finishes it, but hits one dead end or glances at help to get unstuck.
C
Gets there by trial and error — the kind most people abandon before the end.
D
Cannot finish without support, a manual, or someone who has done it before.
F
Most first-time users do not finish at all.
Benchmark

Against best-in-class and the category average — never against your own last year or your own target. “Better than we were” is not a position in a market.

Blind where we can

The experience is scored before the assessor knows whose it is, so the brand’s halo can’t quietly lift its own grade.

Evidence per grade

Every score carries the task, the source and the observation behind it. A grade you can argue with is a grade worth having.

Read across each row. The price row is dark the whole way across — you, the category and the leader all fail it, which is exactly what a category-wide weakness looks like: not a disadvantage, just the cost of being in this business. Now find the row that drops in one column only. Ease of use is deep for the category and the leader and a shade paler for you — a full grade behind on the thing customers touch every day. That single lighter cell, not the dark row, is the priority.

The priority

{{OUTCOME_STAT_LABEL}} — a thorough audit will always hand you nine things to fix. The deliverable was the one relative gap worth closing this year, and the discipline to leave the other eight alone.
